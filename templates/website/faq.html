General Questions

Notely is a simple, elegant note-taking application that allows you to capture and organize your thoughts, ideas, and information. With Notely, you can create, edit, and manage notes from any device with internet access.

Yes, Notely is completely free to use with all core features included. We may introduce premium features in the future, but the basic functionality will always remain free.

Account Questions

To update your profile or change your password:

  1. Click on your name in the top-right corner of the page
  2. Select "Profile" to update your personal information
  3. Select "Change Password" if you want to update your password

You can also recover your password using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

Notes Questions

To create a new note, simply:

  1. Log in to your Notely account
  2. Click the "New Note" button in the navigation bar
  3. Enter a title and content for your note
  4. Click "Create Note" to save

Your note will be instantly saved to your account and accessible from any device.

By default, all notes are private and can only be seen by you. When you make a note public:

  • It becomes visible in the public notes section of the app
  • Anyone with the direct link can view it
  • The note content will be visible without requiring a login

You can toggle a note between private and public at any time from the note's edit page.

Security Questions

Yes, we take data security very seriously. Here's how we protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted via secure HTTPS connections
  • Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Private notes are only accessible to you when logged in

If you have specific security concerns, please contact us.
